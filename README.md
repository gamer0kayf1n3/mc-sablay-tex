# UP Sablay Texture Pack

Experience the University of the Philippines General Commencement Exercise Ceremony right inside Minecraft.

To wear the Sablay, equip any iron chestplate. The texture has been replaced to the Sablay draped over your right shoulder. To flip the Sablay, equip any golden chestplate. The texture has been replaced to the Sablay draped over your left shoulder.

Sablay skin transformed from werhu - UP Sablay - Male (at minecraftskins.com)
